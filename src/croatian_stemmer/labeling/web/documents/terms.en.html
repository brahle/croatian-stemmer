On this site volunteers label Croatian words in sentences taken from language corpora: they judge whether two words share a lemma, confirm or correct lemmas, and judge proposed stems. The answers are used to evaluate and improve an open Croatian stemmer and are published as an open dataset. By accepting these terms you agree to take part on the conditions below.

1. Who may take part

  • People aged 18 or over. Profiles of younger people are erased as soon as we learn of them.
  • Each person may have only one profile.

2. What participation is

Participation is voluntary and unpaid. It creates no employment or any other relationship that would entitle you to payment. You can stop at any time and erase your profile on the "My data" page.

3. Rules of conduct

  • Answer honestly and to the best of your knowledge, following the labeling guidelines.
  • When you are not sure, choose "Not sure". Such an answer is useful and does not count as a mistake.
  • Answer yourself. Answering with scripts, programs, automated tools or artificial-intelligence systems is not allowed.
  • Opening several profiles, sharing a profile with others or using someone else's profile is not allowed.
  • Circumventing the service's limits, disrupting it or trying to access other people's data is not allowed.

4. What the project lead may do

  • Assess how reliable answers are and give them more or less weight in the dataset accordingly.
  • Set a profile aside temporarily (its answers are then disregarded until a person reviews them), exclude a profile's answers from the dataset, or block a profile that breaks these terms. If you disagree with such a decision, write to the contact address.
  • Limit the number of answers per minute and per day, pause the collection of answers, and change or end the project.
  • Publish a dataset containing your answers on the terms of section 5. Published data never contains your name or email address.
