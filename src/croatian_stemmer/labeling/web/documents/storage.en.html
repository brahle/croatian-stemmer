Cookies and browser storage

Version 1.1 (17 October 2026). Controller: {{controller_name}}, {{controller_email}}.

In short: this site uses no cookies and stores nothing permanently in your browser. Short-lived records exist only while you sign in and while you stay signed in within a tab, and they are necessary for the sign-in you asked for. That is why we show no cookie consent banner: strictly necessary storage needs no consent (Article 5(3) of the ePrivacy Directive 2002/58/EC and the Croatian law implementing it).

1. This site ({{app_host}})

  • Cookies: we set none.
  • Sign-in tokens are kept only in the memory of the open page. They disappear when you close the tab, reload the page or sign out.
  • Signed-in marker: after you sign in, the page saves one record named labeling-signed-in with the value 1 in the tab's session storage (sessionStorage). It holds no token and nothing about you; it only tells the page, when you reload it, to ask the sign-in server whether you are still signed in, so a reload does not sign you out. The browser deletes it when you close the tab, and the page deletes it when you sign out or your sign-in has expired. Other tabs do not see it.
  • Sign-in return record: when you click "Sign in", the sign-in library (Keycloak JS) saves one record whose name starts with kc-callback- in the browser's local storage (localStorage). It holds random one-time values used to check that you are returning from a sign-in you started yourself (state, nonce and a PKCE verifier). The record is deleted as soon as you return from signing in. If you abandon the sign-in, it expires after one hour and is deleted on your next visit. When it starts, the library also briefly writes and immediately deletes a test record kc-test. If local storage is disabled in your browser, the same short-lived record is kept in a cookie of this site for at most one hour.
  • Your language choice is not saved in the browser. Choosing English adds ?lang=en to the page address, so a reload keeps it; Croatian is the default.

Purpose: to carry out the sign-in you requested. Strictly necessary; no consent needed.

2. Sign-in server ({{identity_host}})

Sign-in is handled by a Keycloak server at {{identity_host}}. When you sign in, it sets the cookies it needs for signing in on its own address:

NamePurposeLifetime
AUTH_SESSION_ID, KC_AUTH_SESSION_HASHlink the steps of one sign-inuntil the browser is closed
KC_RESTARTlets an interrupted sign-in continueuntil the browser is closed
KEYCLOAK_IDENTITY, KEYCLOAK_SESSIONremember that you are signed in, so you do not have to sign in on every pageuntil you sign out or the sign-in session ends (by default at most 10 hours, or 30 minutes of inactivity)

Some server versions also set the same cookies with a _LEGACY suffix for older browsers. Names can differ slightly between versions; all of them serve sign-in only. Strictly necessary; no consent needed.

3. Google

Signing in with a Google account happens on Google's pages (accounts.google.com), where Google sets its own cookies. Google is responsible for them as an independent controller. See policies.google.com/technologies/cookies.

4. How to remove these records

Signing out ends your session on the sign-in server. You can also delete all records at any time in your browser settings (clear site data).

5. Changes

If we ever start storing anything else in your browser, we will first update this notice, and we will ask for your consent to anything that is not strictly necessary.
